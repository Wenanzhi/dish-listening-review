REVIEW PROTOCOL · V1

Agree on the rules before formal review.

This tool supports sample-based checks of audio and annotation quality. Measuring human task performance requires a separate experiment.

Two-stage workflow

01 · Blind listening

Use only the information allowed by the original question. Listen to the full mixture and record whether the target is clear, partly discernible, indiscernible, or absent from the queried region. Add concerns and time intervals.

02 · Reference check

After you lock stage 1, the reference answer, target identity, original source clips and scene trajectories are revealed. Check their correspondence and independently rate the four review dimensions. Stage 1 cannot be overwritten.

Noise, reverberation, occlusion and overlapping speech may be valid sources of difficulty. Indiscernible speech does not imply an annotation error. Empty-region questions may still contain speech from other regions.

Four review dimensions

Dimension What to check
Audio & rendering Unexpected breaks, repetition, clipping or abrupt changes during motion. Ordinary noise and reverberation are not automatically defects.
Speech & transcript Check that source crops match their transcripts, with no omitted words or answers taken from another speaker.
Question & target Check that content or spatial cues identify a clear target, and that the reference answer belongs to that target.
Space & time Check for obvious anomalies in direction or motion continuity, and whether speech content and time windows match across receivers.

Playback and evidence limits

The original audio is 16 kHz ACN/SN3D [W,Y,Z,X]. The workbench performs binaural convolution using a first-order projection of measured MIT KEMAR HRTFs, with the head aligned to the current receiving robot. All receivers share one playback gain, without per-recording loudness normalization. Convolution tails are retained.

The left/right headphone test checks the playback chain. Directional calibration sounds use the same FOA decoder as the mixtures. Generic HRTFs do not guarantee accurate front/back or elevation judgments for every listener. Exact angles, including 20° thresholds, distances and world coordinates require geometric and rendering checks.

The lossless source clips in stage 2 come from the original speech, with timestamps on the mixture timeline. They are dry source crops; isolated room-rendered source components are not provided. Reference trajectories visualize annotations and are not independent measurements.

Reviewers should be familiar with spoken English and may write their own transcript or describe concerns. The interface locks stage 1 ratings, but a public static website cannot prevent deliberate inspection of reference files. Use controlled conditions for a formal study.

Study organization and statistics

Start with a 20-scene pilot, then fix the review rules. A suggested formal starting point is 100 static and 100 dynamic scenes. Three reviewers import the same sample plan, complete their reviews independently, then discuss disagreements. Room coverage and scene quotas do not ensure sufficient coverage of every speaker-count, noise, overlap or motion condition. The coordinator should inspect the exported plan.

The first reviewer exports the sample plan for the other two to import. Each reviewer exports their own results. The coordinator imports all three files under Results & adjudication. For each dimension, raw agreement and nominal Krippendorff’s α are calculated over matching questions in the same plan. A single reviewer or disjoint samples cannot establish agreement between reviewers.

Unable to judge is a nominal category in α and its proportion is also reported separately. Not applicable is treated as missing. Pilot records, prior exposure to the same base scene and playback failures are excluded from formal statistics. Constant ratings make α undefined. Adjudication is stored separately and never replaces ratings made before discussion.

All ratings are stored in this browser, with no automatic server collection. Export JSON regularly: clearing browser data removes local progress. Reviewer IDs do not authenticate identity. Use separate browser profiles on shared devices.

HEADPHONE CHECK

Check your headphone orientation.

Disable system spatial enhancements. Check the left and right channels at a comfortable volume, then listen to directional sounds generated by the same decoder.

Left and right should be distinguishable. If front and back remain ambiguous, you may still check speech content, but mark the spatial dimension as Unable to judge.
